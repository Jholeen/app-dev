
# REACHER (TV Series)

## About the Series

**Reacher** is an American action, crime, and thriller television series available on *Amazon Prime Video*. It is based on the Jack Reacher novels written by **Lee Child**.

The story follows **Jack Reacher**, a former military police investigator who travels across the United States and becomes involved in dangerous criminal investigations.

## Main Characters

1. **Jack Reacher** - A former military police investigator.
2. **Frances Neagley** - Reacher's trusted friend and former colleague.
3. **Oscar Finlay** - A detective who works with Reacher.
4. **Roscoe Conklin** - A police officer who helps Reacher.
5. **Karla Dixon** - A former member of Reacher's military investigation unit.
